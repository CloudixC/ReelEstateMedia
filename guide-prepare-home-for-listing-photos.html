How to get a home ready for listing photos

A tidy, bright home photographs faster and looks bigger. Send this to your seller a couple of days before the shoot.

The day before

  • Clear the surfaces. Kitchen counters, bathroom vanities, coffee tables and nightstands — leave one or two things on each, put the rest away.
  • Replace dead bulbs and match them where you can; a mix of warm and cold bulbs in one room shows in photos.
  • Clean what the camera sees: windows, mirrors, glass shower doors, stainless steel and floors.
  • Make it less personal. Family photos, fridge magnets, mail and anything with a name on it can come down for the day.
  • Deal with the outside. Mow or shovel, coil the hose, hide the bins and move toys and tools out of sight.

Room by room

Kitchen

Empty the sink and the drying rack, put away small appliances you don’t use daily, and take dish soap, sponges and tea towels off the counter.

Bathrooms

Toilet lids down, fresh towels hung straight, and every bottle, razor and toothbrush in a drawer. Take the bath mat up and hide the bin.

Living and dining rooms

Straighten cushions and throws, put remotes and chargers away, tuck cords behind furniture and push dining chairs in evenly.

Bedrooms

Make the beds tightly, clear the nightstands and keep clothes, laundry baskets and anything under the bed out of view.

Outside

Move cars off the driveway and away from the front of the house. Open patio umbrellas, straighten outdoor furniture and take covers off the barbecue and seating if the weather allows.

On the day

  • Every light on — lamps, under-cabinet lights and outside lights included.
  • Blinds and curtains open unless the view is one you’d rather not show.
  • Ceiling fans and TVs off.
  • Pets out of the way, with bowls, beds and litter boxes put away.
  • Do a last lap with a cloth and an empty laundry basket — anything that doesn’t belong goes in the basket and into the car.

What you can leave alone

The inside of closets, cupboards and drawers isn’t photographed. If a room is being used for storage during the move, tell your agent and it can be skipped.

Good to know

The details, upfront.

  • Flat rates — the same price anywhere in the Greater Toronto Area. Travel beyond it may add a small fee, quoted upfront.
  • Prices are in Canadian dollars and exclude HST.
  • Turnaround — photos in about 3 business days, video in about 5. Rush options are available.
  • Custom packages and volume pricing are welcome — just ask.

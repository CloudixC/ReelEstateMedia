A month of content from one shoot day

Posting regularly is hard when every video needs its own afternoon. Filming several in one session fixes that.

Why batch

Getting camera-ready, travelling and setting up takes the same effort for one reel as for five. Filming a set in one session means you post for weeks from a single morning, and the videos look consistent because they were shot together.

What to film

  • Who you are. One reel that says who you help and where, the one you pin to your profile.
  • Your area. A favourite street, a coffee shop, a park. Neighbourhood content is what locals share.
  • One buyer tip and one seller tip. A question you answer every week, answered once on camera.
  • A market note. What you are seeing right now, in plain words.
  • Something personal. People choose an agent they feel they know.

Before the day

  • Write one line per reel, not a script, just the point you want to land.
  • Bring two or three outfits so the reels don’t all look like the same day.
  • Pick locations close together. Time spent driving is time not filming.
  • Prefer to read your lines? A teleprompter session lets you talk to camera without memorising anything.

How many, and how often

Dynamic Shoot · Tier 1 · 5 ReelsOne session: about a month at one reel a week
Dynamic Shoot · Tier 1 · 10 ReelsTwo sessions: two or three a week for a month
Podcast / Sitdown Shoot · Tier 2 · 5 ReelsPodcast-style or teleprompter reels recorded in our studio
You Shoot, We Edit · 5 ReelsYou send the footage, we edit, no shoot

A steady reel a week beats five in one week and then silence. Spread them out, and save one for a slow week.

After the shoot

Each cut lands in your review room, where you can pin a note at the exact second you want changed. Approved reels are in your hub, ready to download and post.

Walkthrough, listing video or reel: which one does your listing need?

They are all video, but they do different jobs. Pick by where the video will be watched and how much the listing has to show.

Start with where it will be watched

Video for Instagram and TikTok is watched on a phone, held upright, usually with the sound off and often for only a few seconds. Video on a listing page or sent to a seller is watched more deliberately. The first kind needs to grab attention; the second can take its time.

The four kinds we make

Essential Walkthrough Video30s vertical — a quick, upright tour made for social feeds
Professional Listing Video30s cinematic — a polished short cut of the home
Cinematic Listing Video60s cinematic — room to show the layout and the best features
Luxury Property Film60s+ film with a social reel, drone and neighbourhood footage

Which to choose

  • A condo or starter home that will sell on photos: the walkthrough. It gives you something to post the day it lists.
  • A family home with a layout worth showing: a cinematic listing video. Sixty seconds lets a buyer follow the house from the door to the yard.
  • A property where the setting is the selling point: the property film. Drone and neighbourhood footage show what photos of the rooms can’t.
  • Already have footage? A listing reel cut is a second edit from what was shot, sized for social.

Booking photos as well? The listing bundles pair a photo set with video and are usually priced lower than the same pieces on their own.

Make the video work twice

One shoot can feed more than one post: the full video for the listing, a shorter cut for your feed, and stills for a “just listed” graphic. Your hub has tools for the graphics and captions, so the video doesn’t sit in a folder after launch day.

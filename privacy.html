Privacy

What we collect, and why.

Plain answers. This page covers thecontenthub.ca and the listing pages agents publish with it.

Who we are

The Content Hub is run by Reel Estate Media, a real-estate photo and video studio in the Greater Toronto Area, Ontario, Canada. For anything on this page, write to hello@reelestatemedia.com.

What you give us

When you send the enquiry form

Your name, the phone number or email you enter, and your message. It goes to the studio’s inbox and to the studio’s email, and we use it to reply to you. We don’t add you to a mailing list.

When you book a shoot

Booking is handled by Calendly. What you type there is covered by Calendly’s own privacy policy; we receive the booking details so we can show up and do the work.

When you ask for a showing or sign in at an open house on a listing page

Listing pages are published by the agent selling the home. The name and contact details you enter are sent to that agent so they can answer you, and stored by us on the agent’s behalf. Questions about a listing are best sent to the agent named on the page.

When you are a client with a hub

Your hub holds what we need to work together: your bookings, your projects, the files we deliver, and the messages and notes you send. It is private to you and the studio, behind your sign-in.

What the site counts on its own

We keep simple counts of how the site is used: which kind of page was opened, whether a Book or Text button was tapped, and the kind of place a visit came from (for example Instagram or Google). These are totals only. We don’t use advertising trackers or tracking cookies, and we don’t build a profile of you. The site keeps a small marker in your browser for the length of your visit so one visit isn’t counted twice.

Services that help run the site

  • GitHub Pages hosts the pages. Like any web host, it processes technical details such as your IP address in order to deliver them.
  • Google stores the studio’s inbox and files (Apps Script, Sheets and Drive) and serves the fonts and many of the photos and videos you see.
  • Calendly handles bookings.
  • Instagram, YouTube, TikTok and Vimeo open in their own sites when you tap a link to them.

What we don’t do

We don’t sell or rent your information, and we don’t send marketing email you didn’t ask for.

How long we keep it, and your choices

We keep enquiries and client records for as long as they are needed to reply to you, do the work and keep ordinary business records. You can ask what we hold about you, ask us to correct it, or ask us to delete it — email hello@reelestatemedia.com and we’ll answer.

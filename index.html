Good to know

Questions agents ask.

Do I have to be with Royal LePage Pinnacle to book?

No. Any agent in the Greater Toronto Area can book online. Pinnacle agents see our flat rates in their hub; everyone else can ask and we’ll send a quote.

How much does it cost?

Every service is a flat rate. If you’re with Royal LePage Pinnacle, the full price list is in your hub under Book → Services & pricing. Otherwise, tap “Ask a question” below and we’ll send you a quote.

How fast do I get my photos and video?

Photos in about 3 business days, video in about 5. Rush options are available — ask when you book.

Is there a fee for weekends or early and late shoots?

Yes — a $50 fee applies to weekend shoots and to shoots between 6–8 am or 6–9 pm. Weekday shoots in regular hours have no extra fee.

Where do you shoot?

Listings, events and Dynamic Shoots are filmed on location anywhere in the GTA. Podcast / Teleprompter reels and headshots are recorded in our studio in Oakville.

Can I ask for changes?

Yes. You watch the cut in your review room and pin a note at the exact second you want changed.

I already film my own content. Can you just edit it?

Yes — that’s our You Shoot, We Edit service. Send us your footage and we cut a polished reel of up to 30 seconds.

Do I need an account to book?

No — book online like any calendar. Your hub is where you review and download your work, and we set it up for you.

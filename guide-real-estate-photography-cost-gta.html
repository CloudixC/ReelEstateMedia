What real estate photography costs in the GTA

Prices vary from one studio to the next, but they move for the same few reasons. Here is what changes the number, and what we charge.

What changes the price

  • How many photos. A condo needs fewer frames than a large detached home, and most studios price by the size of the set.
  • Video, and how much of it. A short vertical walkthrough costs less than a longer edited film with drone and neighbourhood footage.
  • Extras. Drone, twilight, floor plans, a 3D tour and virtual staging are usually priced separately.
  • Turnaround. Standard delivery is included; a rush is often extra.
  • Travel. Some studios add a fee by distance.

The same price applies anywhere in the Greater Toronto Area; travel beyond it may add a small fee, quoted upfront. Photos are delivered in about 3 business days and video in about 5, with rush options available. Build your shoot to see a total for your listing.

Questions worth asking any photographer

  • How many edited photos do I get, and can I choose them?
  • When will I have them? Ask for the standard turnaround, not the best case.
  • Where can I use them? MLS, your website, print and social media — get the answer before you book.
  • Is tax included? And is there a travel fee to this address?
  • What happens if the weather is bad on the day?
